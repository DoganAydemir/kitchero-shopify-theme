Kitchero 1.0.0 — initial Theme Store release. Online Store 2.0 theme
built on Shopify's Skeleton theme (layouts, critical CSS, CSS variables
and meta tags), with original sections, snippets, styles and scripts,
translations for
English, Turkish, German, French and Spanish, and app block support
on the product page, featured product, header, footer and cart page.

## 1.0.0

### Added

- Home page preset with 15 sections: slideshow, stats, collection
  list, image with text, image with features, steps, before/after
  image, logo list, rich text with image, hotspot image, promotion
  banner, blog posts, testimonials and a parallax call to action.
- Two product page layouts: the standard product page and a premium
  product page with buy buttons, consultation request buttons and an
  optional sample product box.
- Cart drawer with a free shipping progress bar, discount code field,
  gift wrapping request and complementary product recommendations
  from the Shopify Search & Discovery app.
- Predictive search overlay with suggested searches.
- Customer account templates: login, register, account, order,
  addresses, activate account and reset password.
- Low stock message on product pages and cards, shown only when
  inventory tracked by Shopify is at or below the threshold you set.
- Countdown, rating, features, origin, dimensions and swatch data
  read from metafields you choose in Theme settings > Product data.
- Seven color schemes; every section picks its scheme in its own
  settings.
- Theme settings for logo, favicon, page width, spacing, motion,
  intro loader, buttons, inputs, product and collection cards,
  badges, swatches, social links, search, cart, localization
  selectors and optional features.
- Scroll animations load only on screens 750px and wider when the
  visitor hasn't turned on reduced motion, and are skipped on the
  cart, gift card, password, 404, policy and customer pages.
- Predictive search and the cart drawer scripts load when they are
  first needed.
- The first product image, collection banner and slideshow images load
  first; other images load lazily.
- Skip to content link on every page, visible focus styles, and text
  colors that meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on the included color
  schemes.
- Sections update live in the theme editor, and empty sections show
  a hint in the editor about what to add.
